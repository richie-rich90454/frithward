# Code of conduct

Frithward is a welcoming, respectful community, and the work itself is sensitive. People affected by scams, deepfakes, or privacy exposure may be part of any discussion, so tone matters.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent and focus on the work and the safety outcome.
- Respect privacy. Never share victims' private information or non-consensual identity material, including in issues, pull requests, screenshots, or test fixtures.
- Present detection output as evidence with limitations, not as a verdict, and do not mock, blame, or accuse people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Make room for developers, researchers, educators, parents, older adults, translators, journalists, and accessibility advocates.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, publication of private information, deliberate misrepresentation of results, and tools whose primary purpose is impersonating real individuals are not permitted in project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any event or communication where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this standard, and may restrict participation when needed. Reports can be made privately to the maintainers through the [repository](https://github.com/richie-rich90454/frithward). Security vulnerabilities go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as the situation allows.
