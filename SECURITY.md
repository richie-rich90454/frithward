# Security policy

Frithward handles media that can be personal and sensitive. We take reports seriously and ask that you help us fix problems before they are widely known.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities privately. Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability.

Use the repository's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/richie-rich90454/frithward/security/advisories/new), or contact the maintainers through the [repository](https://github.com/richie-rich90454/frithward) if that channel is unavailable. Do not include real victims' data in a report; use synthetic or consented material to reproduce the issue.

Include as much of the following as you can:

- the affected version, commit, or component
- reproduction steps and a minimal proof of concept
- the impact, and who could be harmed
- suggested mitigation, if you have one

## What to expect

The maintainers will acknowledge the report, investigate it, prepare a fix, and publish a coordinated disclosure with a credit line unless you ask to remain anonymous. Security fixes take priority over normal discussion, but they still require tests and follow the release policy in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Scope

Reports about the web app, desktop shell, browser extension, Hono services, Python engines, Rust crates, and the dependency and model supply chain are in scope. Issues that depend on a user intentionally sharing their own credentials, or on modifying their own local installation, are generally out of scope.

Please do not attempt to access, modify, or exfiltrate other users' data while testing. Stop at the boundary needed to demonstrate the issue.
